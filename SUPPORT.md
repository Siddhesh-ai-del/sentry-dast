# Support

## Getting help

sentry-dast is in a pre-release stage. The following channels are available for questions and discussion:

- **Questions and ideas:** open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/Siddhesh-ai-del/sentry-dast/issues) using the appropriate template.
- **Security concerns:** follow the private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly.

## Service level

There is **no service-level agreement (SLA)** at this stage. Responses are provided on a best-effort basis.
